Voting Gate Explorer

n cameras each say yes or no on every scan. A threshold gate flags ERROR when at least k of them say yes. Move the sliders and watch how one camera, an OR of all cameras (k = 1), and a vote compare.

k = 1 is a plain OR of all cameras. k = n is an AND.
